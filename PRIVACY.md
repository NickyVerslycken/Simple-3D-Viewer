# Privacy policy

*Simple 3D Viewer, effective 23 October 2026.*

Simple 3D Viewer is a program that runs entirely on your own computer. **It does not collect, store or send any personal data.**

## What the app does with your data

- **Your 3D files:** the app reads only the files you choose to open (and files that belong to them in the same folder, such as an `.mtl`
  material file or textures). They are read locally and are never uploaded or shared.
- **Settings:** your preferences (color, display mode, shading, background, shadows, grid, whether the side panel is shown, and the
  "Z axis is up" choice per file type) are saved on your device, in the app's local storage, so they are there the next time you start the app. They are never sent anywhere.
- **Screenshots:** a PNG file is written only when you press the screenshot button, to the place you choose.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
- No internet connection is needed or used by the app itself. When you choose **Help → Project on GitHub** (or similar), your web browser opens that page.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.
