# Example models

Small models to try the viewer with. They were generated by `test/make-samples.mjs` and may be used freely.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `gear.stl` | A single STL part with sharp edges and round holes. Try *Edges*, *Flat* shading and *Wire*. |
| `knot-assembly.obj` + `knot-assembly.mtl` | Three separate parts with colors in the material file. Try *Color: By part* and *Color: File* (keep both files together in one folder). |
| `two-parts.3mf` | A 3MF plate with two colored parts. Try *Color: File*. |

Open one with **Open** in the app, drag it onto the window, or double-click it once the app is your default viewer.
